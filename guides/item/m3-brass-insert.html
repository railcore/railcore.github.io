M3 Brass Insert

An outer knurled and inner threaded brass insert that is heated and then embedded in a plastic printed part to allow mechanical fasteners to be used to secure parts together.

Used in

  • RailCore II: B) Prep
  • RailCore II: K) Y Carriage
  • RailCore® Mini Kit: B) Prep
