About this archive

Dozuki ended its free plan and turned railcore.dozuki.com off on 2026-11-20. This is a full static copy made through Dozuki's public API before the shutdown: every guide, step, bullet, part and tool page, all 300 steps' images at their original upload resolution, and the 120 reader comments.

  • Text and images are unchanged. Links between guides and to part pages now point inside this archive.
  • The Dropbox folders the guides linked to (RepRapFirmware config files) are mirrored as zips under files/.
  • Click any step image for the full-resolution original.
  • Each kit has a whole build on one page view for printing or saving as PDF.
