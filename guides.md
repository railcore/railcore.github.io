# RailCore Build Guides

Step-by-step assembly guides for the RailCore II 300ZL and the RailCore Mini, with photos for every step.
They lived on Dozuki until it shut down on 2026-11-20 and are now part of this site.
Click any step photo for the full-resolution original.

Spotted a problem, or want to help tidy the guides up? Join the [#documentation channel on Discord](https://discord.gg/ZgYaFPY).
