---
title: Solid State Relays (SSR)
---

The RailCore II's AC bed heater is switched by a solid state relay (SSR). A 10-15A SSR is required.

You can get cheap chinese SSR's for $2, but it is recommended to buy genuine/certified SSR's from a known source such as RS Components or Farnell for ~$20 range.
These are AC voltages, and you **really** don't want to mess around.

Good makes are Crydom (Sensata-Crydom) and Panasonic. 

In the build guide: mounting is [guide P, step 4](../guides/guide/15-p-electronics.html#s100) and wiring is [guide Q, steps 3, 7-8 and 13](../guides/guide/16-q-wiring-the-electronics.html#s112).
