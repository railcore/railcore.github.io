---
title: Fans
---

For Radial fans for layer cooling, an upgrade from the one in the BOM is the Delta fan model BFB0524HH - they put out a lot of air, and are quieter as well. A nice high quality fan.

In the build guides: the layer fan goes on in guide K ([RailCore II](../guides/guide/9-k-y-carriage.html#s59), [Mini](../guides/guide/33-k-y-carriage.html#s268)) and is wired in guide Q ([RailCore II](../guides/guide/16-q-wiring-the-electronics.html#s130), [Mini](../guides/guide/39-q-wiring-the-electronics.html#s302)). The Mini also has [electronics panel fans](../guides/guide/38-p-electronics.html#s296). For 12V fans on a 24V printer, see [12V fans](../customization/12v_fans.md).
